Questions

Anything else? Ask on WhatsApp or call +353 83 205 9510.

What files can I send?

STL, 3MF and OBJ get an instant price. STEP files are welcome too — we quote those by hand, usually within a few hours. Files up to 25 MB, and parts up to 256 mm on each side (bigger parts can be printed in pieces).

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still help?

Most everyday parts already exist as free models on Printables, MakerWorld or Thingiverse. If you need something designed from scratch, message us and we'll recommend a designer we trust.

PLA or PETG?

PLA suits most things: clean finish and accurate, for indoor parts, prototypes and models. PETG is stronger and handles heat better, for parts under stress or in warmer places. PETG is +€1 per order.

Is the instant price the final price?

Yes. A person checks every file before printing, and the price only changes if the file has a problem — we always tell you first.

How do I pay?

By Revolut or bank transfer. We send the details when we confirm your order.

Do you post outside Cork?

Yes, anywhere in Ireland with An Post. It's €5 for small parts and €9 for bigger ones, and the price box shows which applies before you order. We post the day it's ready and An Post usually delivers the next working day. Or collect for free in Cork City centre.

Do you remove the supports?

Yes, for free — or we leave them on if you prefer, which helps with delicate parts. You can also ask for a free timelapse video of your print.
