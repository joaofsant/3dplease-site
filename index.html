How it works

Three simple steps from your idea to a printed part in your hand.

Step 01
Send your file or describe your part

Upload an STL, STEP, or OBJ via WhatsApp or email. We'll get back to you with a quote.

Step 02
Get a fixed quote and confirm

We reply with a fixed price and ready time. No surprises. Payment via Revolut or cash on collection. We only start once you confirm.

Step 03
Collect in Cork City

Pick up in Cork City centre. We confirm the exact address when your order is placed. Postal delivery available on request.

Clean finish, dimensionally accurate, easy to post-process. Great for prototypes, display models, and everyday indoor parts.
