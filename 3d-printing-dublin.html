Questions

Anything else? Ask on WhatsApp or call +353832059510.

How long does delivery to Dublin take?

Standard prints are ready in 2–3 days. If you choose Next day in the quote, it's included for prints under 4 hours (+€3 for longer ones, when there's capacity). We post it the day it's ready, and An Post usually delivers to Dublin the next working day.

How much is postage to Dublin?

€5 if the whole order weighs up to 250 g and its longest side is up to 200 mm, €9 for anything bigger. The price box adds the right one, so you see the full total before you order.

Can you post to an office or college address?

Yes, any Dublin address with an Eircode. Put a name and a note on the order so it reaches the right desk or room.

Can I collect in Dublin?

No, we don't have a pickup point in Dublin. Everything is printed in Cork and sent by An Post. If you're ever in Cork, collection in the city centre is free — choose "Collect in Cork" in the quote.

What files can I send?

STL, 3MF, OBJ and STEP all get an instant price. Files up to 25 MB, and parts up to 256 mm on each side. Bigger parts can be printed in sections for you to put together. If a file can't be read automatically, send it anyway and we'll price it by hand.

How do I pay?

By Revolut or bank transfer. A person checks your file first, then we send the payment details with the order confirmation.
